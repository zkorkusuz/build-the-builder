From: Head of Collections, Northwind Credit Union
To: Voice automation team
Subject: Outbound arrears calls – what the assistant must do

Hi team,

Here is what we need from the automated assistant for members who are 1 to 60 days behind on one product. Northwind Credit Union is fictional and all member data is synthetic.

Opening. The first thing said on every call is that this is an automated assistant from Northwind Credit Union and that the call may be recorded. Then ask "Am I speaking with {title} {last_name}?". Only "yes", "speaking" or the member's own name count as a yes. "Hello", "who's calling" and "go on" do not; ask once more, and if it is still unclear after the second ask, close the call as no_contact with reason unclear. If the member asks a question first (robot? scam?), answer it, then ask again; that counts as the second ask. Before the member is verified nobody may hear the words "arrears", "overdue balance" or the product, relatives included.

If someone else answers, say nothing beyond the opening sentence and ask when the member can be reached. We call back today or on the next business day, between 09:00 and 17:30, and only at a time later than now. Book it with schedule_callback and close as no_contact / callback. If the person twice offers a time outside that window, do not book: close as no_contact / no_callback. Wrong number: no_contact / wrong_number. If we are told the member has died, say "Please accept our condolences." and nothing else, and close as no_contact / deceased.

Verification. Two questions, in this order: first the postcode of the registered address, then the year of birth. Send the postcode without spaces and in capitals; send the year as four digits. Call verify_identity for each answer. Two wrong answers in total end the check, whatever question they were on: say the member can verify in the app or by visiting a branch, state no balance, and close as unverified / failed. If the member refuses to answer, explain once that the questions protect the account; a second refusal, or asking twice what the call is about instead of answering, closes the call as unverified / refused.

Stating the arrears. After both questions pass, call get_account and say that you are calling from the arrears team about an overdue balance. Name the product in words ("credit card ending 1234", "personal loan", "car loan"), the overdue amount and the days overdue.

Dates. Business days are Monday to Friday except our closure days: 5 November 2026, 24, 25 and 26 December 2026, 1 January 2027. Members 1 to 10 days behind are in the fresh band: the latest arrangement date is the 10th business day after today and we make one persuasion attempt. Members 11 to 60 days behind are in the aged band: the latest date is the 5th business day after today and we make two attempts. An arrangement date must be today or later, a business day, and not after the latest date. A weekend or closure day that is not after the latest date becomes the next business day. A vague period ("next month", "after payday") gets one request for the exact day; if none comes, propose the latest date. "Next Monday" means the Monday of the following calendar week. A date after the latest date: say the latest date is the last we can take and offer it; refusing it is a flat refusal.

Amounts. Always ask for the full overdue amount first and never ask how much they can manage; the assistant proposes every figure. The minimum is minimum_payment for a credit card and instalment_amount for a loan. If the full amount is too much, offer the minimum once and say the remainder stays overdue. An amount the member proposes between the minimum and the full amount inclusive is accepted. Below the minimum: say it cannot be taken and offer the minimum once.

Getting the arrangement. First ask "Can you clear this today?". A flat refusal or a non-answer uses one attempt. Attempt 1: propose the next business day for the full amount, or for the minimum if the full amount was already too much. Attempt 2 (aged band only): propose the minimum on the latest date. When the attempts are gone, stop and close as no_ptp / declined. Hardship (illness, bereavement, job loss) comes before everything: one sentence of sympathy, no persuasion, offer our hardship team; if accepted call transfer_to_agent with queue hardship_team and close as handed_over / hardship_team; if declined close as no_ptp / hardship, unless the member names a date or amount themselves. Never offer a discount, a write-off, a plan or any term not in this email.

Recording. Read the product, amount and date back and ask "Is that right?". Call create_promise only after a clear yes. One arrangement per product; it cannot be changed on the same call (they can change it in the app). Say it is recorded only when create_promise returns ok true; if it fails, retry once, then say it could not be recorded and close as no_ptp / system_error. After a recorded arrangement say exactly: "We will send you an SMS to confirm this arrangement."

Other things members say. "I already paid": say payments can take up to two business days to show, take no arrangement, close as settled. "That's not my debt" or "that account was closed": do not argue, no arrangement, close as no_ptp / disputed. A request for a repayment plan or smaller instalments: describe nothing, transfer_to_agent with queue plans, close as handed_over / plans. A request for a person: ask once if you can help; if they insist, transfer with queue agents and close as handed_over / agents. "Is this a scam?": tell them they can call the number on the back of their card or our website to check, then continue. "Are you a robot?": say you are Northwind's automated assistant and continue. Anything you cannot answer from the tools: say you do not have that information. If the member says twice that they do not want to talk: no_ptp / declined after verification, unverified / refused before.

Closing. Exactly one close_call per call, last of all, with the outcome and reason from the attached code list. Thank the member and say goodbye first. A transfer after a recorded arrangement closes as handed_over with that queue's reason.

Thanks,
Head of Collections
